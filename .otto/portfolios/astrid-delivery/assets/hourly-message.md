# Hourly orchestrator check

Follow [operations](../operations.md), [checkpoint histories](../checkpoint-history.md) and [the orchestration contract](../orchestrator-contract.md). These are the canonical constraints; repeat critical source/authority boundaries for a new recipient or risky handoff, not the entire unchanged prompt at every check.

## Checklist

1. **Recover state and time.** Read the compact status/thread map and previous checkpoint decisions. Count actual elapsed hours and completed checks separately; report the next 12-hour boundary. Verify the destination GPU/UE owner and actual task rather than infer P7/R7/T7 completion.
2. **One Luna check per active project.** Give each investigator the shared checkpoint ID, original tasklist and previous history/action. Stagger them for Mac capacity; reuse the same project's in-flight check. Include quiet projects: little information and justified waiting are acceptable. Dormant projects need only readiness/dependency checking. No extra output, tests or deep research merely to make a quiet report look substantial.
3. **Compare plan, progress and prior decisions.** Investigators record evidence-backed change versus the expected milestone, exact source/time, claims versus facts, blockers and whether the previous action helped. Look for duplicate workers/tests, shared-file conflicts, scope drift, ineffective repeated actions or missed milestones. Quietness and raw commit counts are not performance measures.
4. **Check resources and ready work.** Account for the GPU owner's load, test children/services, RAM/paging, CPU responsiveness, disk trend and review/provider balances. Follow shared heavy-job admission and safe owned cleanup. Identify a genuinely ready next project/disjoint task; overlapping work retains the accepted checkpoint/main/dirty composition gate. Investigators inspect receipts rather than automatically rerun suites.
5. **Drill down only where useful.** For a concrete discrepancy, ask the assigned Luna to inspect the relevant task brief, worker result/conversation tail and source/receipt. Return observation, evidence, cause or uncertainty, and smallest useful action. Do not copy full transcripts, create a panel or resume a live owner in another process. Missing monitoring alone does not block otherwise-proven work; missing mandatory proof or a material unresolved contradiction still can.
6. **Run the due 12-hour judgment.** Follow [periodic course correction](../periodic-course-correction.md): supply overall plan and full history access, including prior correction outcomes. Quiet intervals do not waive this call—they may reveal the most important bottleneck. Coalesce missed intervals, preserve separate accounting and do not add another test campaign.
7. **Judge and act.** Choose continue, unblock, redirect, await a named event or hold affected work. Send the existing manager a message only for changed assignment/priority, new relevant evidence, a concrete unresolved question or a warranted intervention. Do not send repetitive unchanged nudges. Trust routine judgment; consequential disputes use the configured oracle within authority. If the same decision recurs twice without useful action/evidence, repair the coordination cause rather than repeat the same research.
8. **Record once, report and await.** Append your actual decision after the investigator finishes its observation. Astra low may do small clerical updates directly under the contract; otherwise batch judgments/status updates through one existing recorder assignment. Keep history authoritative for chronology and status a linked current summary. Post meaningful changes, important waiting/resource constraints and next actions in this orchestrator thread; no need to echo every ledger field. Await the next bounded same-thread wakeup-loop delay; do not finish expecting an unawaited callback. Stop only under the actual completion/user-stop/blocker rules.

## Boundaries that still apply

Required tests/reviews, exact-source evidence, exclusive ownership, provider budgets, constrained-Mac admission and protected data remain unchanged. A monitoring report is not an acceptance verdict. Preserve uncertainty and incomplete checks honestly; do not fabricate progress or waive demonstrated failure. Keep independent work moving. No automatic extra agents, repeated full-suite validation, production deployment, force-push, broad cleanup or competing manager threads.
